# Product fact sheet (fictional)

This is the single source of truth for the fictional credit-building app used in Fynn Lab. The skills and personas rely on it, and so will the eval expected answers.

These products are invented for this demo and are not Kikoff's products. General credit facts (how scores work, how long items stay on reports) live in the skills and must be accurate in the real world. The product details below are made up and only need to stay consistent.

The assistant is called **Coach**. The app itself is never named; the assistant says "the app" or "our".

## Credit Builder plan

- $6 per month for 24 months. It works as a small installment loan whose payments build a savings balance.
- Opening it needs no credit check and no hard inquiry.
- Each monthly payment is reported to Equifax, Experian and TransUnion as an installment account.
- Reporting happens once a month, a few days after the payment posts.
- A payment that is 1 to 29 days late is not reported as late. It is reported as late only once it is 30 or more days past due.
- When the 24 months end, the user gets back the savings balance ($144, minus any unpaid amounts).
- Cancelling early closes the account. The closed account stays on the report with its payment history.

## Secured Card

- The refundable security deposit is $50 to $2,000, and the credit limit equals the deposit.
- No hard inquiry is needed to apply, and there is no annual fee. The variable APR is 27.99%.
- Card delivery:
  - The card ships 1 to 2 business days after approval.
  - Delivery takes 7 to 10 business days after shipping.
  - If the card has not arrived 10 business days after shipping, the user can request a replacement in the app (Card > Report not received).
- The card must be activated in the app before first use.
- Each month the statement balance and payment status are reported to Equifax, Experian and TransUnion. Because the statement balance is what gets reported, paying before the statement date lowers reported utilization.
- After 8 consecutive on-time payments, the account is reviewed for an upgrade to an unsecured card. If upgraded, the deposit is returned.

## Marketplace

- The Marketplace shows third-party credit card and loan offers.
- The app may be paid by partners when a user is approved for an offer.
- The app does not know a user's approval odds and never guarantees approval.
- Applying through a partner usually triggers a hard inquiry from that issuer.

## Rent Reporting

- $4 per month.
- On-time rent payments, verified through the user's linked bank account, are reported to TransUnion and Experian. Rent is not reported to Equifax.
- Up to 12 months of past rent can be added for a one-time $25 fee.
- Late rent is never reported. A missed month is simply not reported.
- Whether rent affects a score depends on the scoring model. FICO 8 ignores rent, while FICO 9, FICO 10 and VantageScore 3.0 and 4.0 can count reported rent.

## Bill Reporting

- Free.
- On-time utility, phone and internet bill payments, found in the user's linked bank account, are reported to Equifax.
- Only on-time payments are reported. Late bills are never reported.
- As with rent, the effect depends on the scoring model and on whether a lender pulls Equifax.

## Debt Settlement service

- This service negotiates with creditors on accounts that are charged off or in collections. It does not cover current accounts.
- Fees and payments:
  - The fee is 20% of the amount saved. It is charged only after a settlement is agreed and the user has made at least one payment under it. There are no upfront fees.
  - The user pays the settlement directly to the creditor or through the app. There is no dedicated savings account.
  - Typical outcomes vary widely, and no settlement amount is guaranteed.
- What settlement does not change:
  - A settled account is usually reported as "settled for less than the full balance". Settlement does not remove the account.
  - Negative history stays up to 7 years from the original delinquency date.
- Forgiven debt of $600 or more usually triggers a Form 1099-C, and the forgiven amount may be taxable income. The app does not give tax advice.

## Support

- Users can reach human support in the app (Help > Chat with support) or by email at support@example.com.
