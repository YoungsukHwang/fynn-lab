## Facts
- Lenders usually report monthly, each on its own date, often near the statement closing date. A payoff or new account can take roughly 30 to 45 days to appear.
- Common causes of a move: a different reported balance (see utilization), a new 30+ day late payment (see payment-history), a new hard inquiry, a new account, a closed account, or an older negative item aging. Small swings with no new negative item are normal.
- Hard inquiries stay on a report for 2 years; FICO counts only those from the last 12 months. FICO says one extra inquiry takes fewer than 5 points off for most people.
- Soft inquiries, including checking your own score, do not affect scores.
- A new account lowers the average age of accounts.
- Closing a card can change utilization (see utilization). A closed account in good standing can stay on the report for up to 10 years.
- Bureau scores differ because not every lender reports to all three bureaus, and bureaus update on different days. A gap between scores is expected.
- Models (FICO 8, 9, 10T, VantageScore 3.0 and 4.0, industry auto and bankcard versions) weigh the same data differently. A lender may use a different bureau or model than the score the user sees.

## How to answer
- Compare `credit.score` with any earlier score in the profile, then check `credit.negative_items`, recent events and `credit.utilization`. Name the likely causes, biggest first (a new 30+ day late outweighs an inquiry).
- If nothing in the profile explains the change, say so and list the usual causes.
- For "when will it recover", explain the monthly lag and the lever that fits the cause.
- If the user has the app's rent or bill reporting, note those go to specific bureaus (see rent-reporting, bill-reporting).

## Do not
- Predict how many points, as a number or a range, a change will cost or recover, or when the score will reach a level. Cite FICO's inquiry figure only as a general statement, not as this user's outcome.
- Treat a gap between scores as an error.
- Invent events, dates or accounts that are not in the profile.
